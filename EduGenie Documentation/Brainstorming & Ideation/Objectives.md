# EduGenie – Objectives

## 1. Main Objective

The main objective of EduGenie is to develop an AI-powered educational assistant that provides learners with accessible and personalized learning support through a simple web-based interface.

## 2. Specific Objectives

### Objective 1 – Question Answering

To provide clear and concise answers to students' academic and learning-related questions.

### Objective 2 – Simplified Learning

To simplify complex concepts and present them in an understandable form suitable for the learner's level.

### Objective 3 – Quiz Generation

To generate multiple-choice quizzes that allow learners to practice and assess their understanding of a topic.

### Objective 4 – Summarization

To summarize lengthy educational content into concise and useful material for quick revision.

### Objective 5 – Personalized Learning Guidance

To provide structured learning recommendations based on the learner's topic, level, and learning goal.

### Objective 6 – Integrated Learning Support

To combine multiple learning-support activities within a single application instead of requiring separate tools for every activity.

### Objective 7 – User-Friendly Interaction

To provide a simple web interface through which learners can easily select a learning task, provide input, and view the generated result.

### Objective 8 – AI Integration

To integrate generative AI capabilities into an educational application for interactive and adaptive learning support.
