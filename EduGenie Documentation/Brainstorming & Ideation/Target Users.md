# EduGenie – Target Users

## 1. Overview

EduGenie is designed to support learners who require additional assistance with understanding, practicing, revising, and planning their learning activities.

## 2. Primary Target Users

### 2.1 Students

Students can use EduGenie to:

- Ask academic questions.
- Understand difficult concepts.
- Generate quizzes.
- Summarize study material.
- Explore structured learning paths.

### 2.2 Beginners

Beginners can use simplified explanations and structured learning recommendations to build foundational knowledge before moving towards advanced concepts.

### 2.3 Self-Learners

Self-learners can use EduGenie as an accessible learning companion while exploring new subjects independently.

## 3. Other Potential Users

### 3.1 Educational Institutions

Educational institutions can potentially use similar AI-assisted learning capabilities to support students and supplementary learning activities.

### 3.2 Educational Content Platforms

Educational content platforms can potentially integrate AI-assisted features such as question answering, summarization, quizzes, and learning recommendations.

## 4. Learning Levels

EduGenie can support learners at different levels:

- Beginner
- Intermediate
- Advanced

## 5. User Needs

The major user needs addressed by EduGenie include:

- Quick answers to questions.
- Easy-to-understand explanations.
- Practice through quizzes.
- Quick revision through summaries.
- Structured guidance for further learning.
