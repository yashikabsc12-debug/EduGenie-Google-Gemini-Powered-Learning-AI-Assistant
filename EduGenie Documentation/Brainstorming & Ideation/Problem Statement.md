# EduGenie – Problem Statement

## 1. Background

Students often need additional support while learning different academic topics. They may have doubts, find complex concepts difficult to understand, require quick revision material, or need guidance about what to learn next.

Traditional learning methods may not always provide immediate and personalized support for every learner. Students may need to search through multiple resources to find explanations, practice questions, summaries, and suitable learning guidance.

## 2. Problem Identification

The major problem identified is the lack of a simple and centralized learning assistant that can provide different types of educational support through a single interface.

Students may face difficulties such as:

- Getting immediate answers to academic questions.
- Understanding complex topics in simple language.
- Creating practice quizzes for self-assessment.
- Summarizing lengthy educational content.
- Identifying what topics to learn next.
- Following a structured learning path from beginner to advanced levels.

## 3. Problem Statement

There is a need for an accessible AI-powered educational assistant that can help learners understand topics, answer questions, generate quizzes, summarize educational content, and provide personalized learning recommendations through a single web-based platform.

## 4. Proposed Problem Context

EduGenie is designed to address this problem by providing multiple learning-support features through one application. The system is intended to support learners by simplifying complex concepts, providing question-and-answer assistance, generating quizzes, summarizing educational passages, and creating structured learning recommendations.

## 5. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a simple and user-friendly learning assistant that can provide AI-powered educational support and help learners access different learning activities from a single platform.
