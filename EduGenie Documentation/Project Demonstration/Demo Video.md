# EduGenie – Demo Video

## 1. Demo Video

The demonstration video shows the complete working flow of the EduGenie AI learning assistant.

## 2. Demonstration Covered

The video demonstrates:

- Opening the EduGenie application.
- Question and Answer feature.
- Topic Explanation feature.
- Quiz Generation feature.
- Text Summarization feature.
- Personalized Learning Path feature.
- Interaction between the frontend and backend.
- Generated AI responses.

## 3. Demo Sequence

### Step 1 – Launch Application

Start the FastAPI application and open EduGenie in the browser.

### Step 2 – Question and Answer

Enter an academic question and demonstrate the generated answer.

### Step 3 – Topic Explanation

Enter a topic and demonstrate the simplified explanation.

### Step 4 – Quiz Generation

Enter a topic or learning content and generate a quiz.

### Step 5 – Summarization

Enter a lengthy paragraph and demonstrate the generated summary.

### Step 6 – Learning Path

Enter a topic, learning level, and learning goal and demonstrate the recommended learning path.
